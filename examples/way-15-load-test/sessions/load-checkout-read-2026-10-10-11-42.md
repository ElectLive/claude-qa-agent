## Reading the numbers

`smoke` proved the script works and set the baseline. Under `load`, order
history is the only endpoint that degrades super-linearly: 10.1× its smoke
latency, against 3.6× and 6.4× for the others. That shape — one endpoint
growing much faster than its neighbours — points at something per-user in the
orders handler rather than at the shared checkout stack.

Threshold ✅/❌ above is per endpoint, as reported by the tagged
`http_req_duration{endpoint:…}` metrics, not inferred from the global average.
